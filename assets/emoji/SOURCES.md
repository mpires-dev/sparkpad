# Emoji catalog

Unicode emoji-test.txt version 17.0 from https://unicode.org/Public/17.0.0/emoji/emoji-test.txt; fully-qualified sequences. Portuguese labels and search terms from Unicode CLDR (common/annotations/pt.xml and annotationsDerived/pt.xml). English aliases from https://github.com/muan/emojilib. The generated JSON is embedded and works offline. Unicode and Emojilib licenses are included here and in the app bundle.
